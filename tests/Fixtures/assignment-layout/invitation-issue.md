<details>
<summary><strong>What this is about</strong></summary>
<dl>
<dd>
<br>

A workspace owner cannot invite a colleague today. The owner asks an administrator to create the account by hand, and the colleague waits days. We want the owner to invite a colleague by e-mail, so the colleague joins on their own.

</dd>
</dl>
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>What the user must be able to do</strong></summary>
<dl>
<dd>
<br>

The owner enters a colleague's e-mail on the workspace settings page and sends the invitation. The colleague opens the link from the e-mail, sets a password, and joins the workspace as a member. The owner sees the open invitations and can cancel one.

</dd>
</dl>
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>The rules</strong></summary>
<dl>
<dd>
<br>

An invitation link works once. A link expires after 7 days. An owner sends at most 20 invitations a day. A link that is used, expired, or cancelled shows "This invitation is no longer valid".

</dd>
</dl>
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>What we are not doing now</strong></summary>
<dl>
<dd>
<br>

Inviting several colleagues at once from a file, because the first release proves the single invitation. Choosing the role of the new member, because every invited colleague joins as a member.

</dd>
</dl>
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Open questions for the owner</strong></summary>
<dl>
<dd>
<br>

Should an owner be able to resend an invitation? Until the owner decides, a new invitation to the same e-mail replaces the old one.

</dd>
</dl>
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Dependencies</strong></summary>
<dl>
<dd>
<br>

- None.

</dd>
</dl>
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Original assignment</strong></summary>
<dl>
<dd>
<br>

Let a workspace owner invite colleagues by e-mail. The colleague accepts through a link and joins as a member.

</dd>
</dl>
</details>
